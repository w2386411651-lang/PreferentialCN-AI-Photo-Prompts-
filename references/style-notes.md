# Style Notes

## 常用摄影词

- 商业摄影：commercial photography
- 棚拍：studio photography
- 柔和自然光：soft natural light
- 浅景深：shallow depth of field
- 真实质感：realistic texture
- 电影感：cinematic
- 高级简洁构图：premium minimal composition

## 人像常用补充

```text
realistic skin texture, refined makeup, soft facial expression, natural pose, sharp eyes, elegant styling
```

## 食物常用补充

```text
appetizing texture, clean plate, soft highlights, natural shadows, fresh ingredients, commercial food photography
```

## 产品图常用补充

```text
premium product photography, clean background, soft reflection, precise lighting, sharp edges, luxury composition
```

## 常用负面提示词

```text
low quality, blurry, noise, text, watermark, logo, distorted, deformed, extra fingers, missing fingers, bad anatomy, oversaturated, plastic texture
```
